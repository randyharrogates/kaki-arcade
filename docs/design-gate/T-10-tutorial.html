First-run tutorial

A 60-second playable tutorial the first time a room plays Tabao Rush. It teaches four things in order, one at a time; each step ends when every cook has done it once. The leader can skip it from their phone at any moment. Base kitchen: T-07's real round-12 still at 1920 × 1080 (placeholder geometry, station cues and step words as in T-07). Phone: board 4's controller, 1A buttons, at 844 × 390.
