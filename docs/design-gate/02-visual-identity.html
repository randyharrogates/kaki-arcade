Choice 2

Where trivia photos come from

You asked for real photographs in Rabakz, not Blender renders. That is now the rule (RZ-025). The choice is how the 60 or more picture questions get their photos.

P1

Free real photographs

Bowl of Katong Laksa, by JaventheAlderick, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.

  • It is the real thing, which matters when the photo is the answer: a dish, a landmark, an animal.
  • Free. Thousands exist for Singapore subjects.
  • Every photo needs its licence checked and its credit listed on the credits page.
  • Quality and framing vary from photo to photo.
Recommended for anything factual
P2

Generated with Codex

  • One consistent look and framing across every question.
  • No credits or licences to track.
  • It is not a photograph of a real thing. A generated “laksa” or “Merlion” can be subtly wrong, and a wrong picture makes a wrong question.
  • Every image needs a human check for accuracy.

A mix is possible: real photographs wherever the picture is the fact, generated images only for generic subjects. Say “P1 with P2 for generic”.

Proportions on this board: all three directions use the 5 · 8 · 13 · 21 · 34 · 55 · 89 px spacing scale and 1.618 : 1 splits (lobby join panel to seats, Rabakz question to picture, TV to phone on this page). C is the strict golden-ratio variant: its type sizes are also on the scale (34, 55, 89, 144 px) and its corners are 13 px.
