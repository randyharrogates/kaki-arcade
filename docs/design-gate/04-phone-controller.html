What each player holds

Every phone screen, in the looks you chose, at 844 × 390: an iPhone held sideways. On a laptop the frames are close to life size. The phone only ever shows controls and what is private to that player; everything else is on the TV. Open this on your phone, turn it sideways, and try reaching the buttons with your thumbs.

Reply with One letter for each numbered choice, for example “1B 2B 3A 4B”. The single-design screens need a yes, or what to change. Nothing here costs frame rate: the phone draws a few fixed shapes and sends thumb positions.

Proportions on this board: in the options marked “golden ratio” the screen is split 1 : 1.618 (322 px to 522 px): stick side to button side, text to card grid. Spacing and sizes come from 5 · 8 · 13 · 21 · 34 · 55 · 89 · 144 px; corners are 13 px. Touch targets are at least 55 px, and the main action buttons are 110 px or more. The phone size is fixed; other phones scale the same layout to fit, never scroll.
