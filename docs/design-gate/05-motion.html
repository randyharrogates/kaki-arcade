How things move

Every sample below is live and follows your motion rules. Change the tempo and the easing at the top and all of them replay with the new values. Four choices are needed; this is the last board before building anything visible.

How to reply

  1. Tempo: 1A, 1B or 1C
  2. Easing: 2A or 2B
  3. Card reveal: 3A or 3B
  4. Screen change: 4A or 4B

For example "1B 2A 3A 4A". Those four are my recommendation.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the samples only change state. The games will do the same.

      The floating stick

      Touch and drag anywhere here

      The stick appears where your thumb lands and follows it exactly, with no smoothing while held. The knob stretches about 12% in the direction of travel. On release it eases back and stops, with no bounce. The four buttons are the shapes you chose, in Kaki Arcade's own colours; this is not a choice on this board.
