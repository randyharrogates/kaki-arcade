Seven small fixes to choose

The review of the design tokens measured every text and background pair you chose. Most pass. These do not, and fixing them means touching colours or sizes that are now locked, so each is yours to decide. Every sample is drawn at true size with the measured contrast ratio; body text needs 4.5, large text and symbols need 3.
