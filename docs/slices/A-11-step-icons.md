# A-11 — Recipe-step icons

## Goal and scope

TR-001 (partial): provide the eleven recipe-step glyph assets for order cards.
Original Blender polygon meshes cover chop (cleaver/board), wok (handled pan),
pot (lid and side handles), grill (grate), rice (cooker), drinks (straw cup), mix
(whisk/bowl), spread (knife), plate (flat rim), bowl (deep solid) and cup (handled
outline/saucer). All use unlit print red-brown #8E2A18 on transparent 34×34
canvases. A-05 art and renderer code are untouched. No runtime wiring, tracker
updates, new dependencies, rendering or packing is part of this round.

## Built and acceptance

- `tools/blender/step_icon_geometry.py`, `step_icons.py` and
  `test_step_icon_geometry.py`: pure additive geometry, exact scene-linear emission,
  transparent orthographic HUD rendering, centre anchors and pure tests.
- `assets/src/pipeline.json`: one render job with content-fingerprinted geometry
  and colour helper; lossless `tabao-rush-step-icons` set in the round. Existing
  pipeline creates full and half detail, sourceSize and trim-aware metadata.
- `tools/assets/test_step_icons.py`: registration/stale-input coverage,
  small synthetic metadata packing, source and packed pixel acceptance,
  silhouette distinctness at both details and round budgets including manifest.
- `tools/assets/step_icon_sheets.py`: compact native/4× light/dark and greyscale
  sheets, packed full/half reconstruction, chicken-rice card crops at 1920 and
  1280 with 34 px glyphs and 13 px edge gaps at 1920. No duplicate asset copies.
- `docs/ART-PIPELINE.md` Step icons section documents the consumer placement.

Every one of 55 silhouette pairs must differ on at least 6% of the full 34×34
box (about 70 pixels), alpha >128, after aligning mask bounding boxes, also
after packed half detail is reconstructed at 34 px. An absolute-box floor guards near-duplicate shapes without inflating
scores from tiny occupied unions. This is a regression gate, not proof of
semantic recognition. All pixels with alpha above zero must be within ±6/channel of (142,42,24),
with transparent padding and no clipping; sourceSize/anchor remain 34/[17,17]
(full) and 17/[8.5,8.5] (half). Half is generated by the packer, not rerendered.

  RGBA PNG bytes with every RGB fixed to #8E2A18. The standard-library encoder

  in Blender. A pure test verifies the RGB bytes, alpha values and PNG CRCs.
