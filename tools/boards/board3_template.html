Design gate · board 3 · big-screen layouts

Every TV screen, laid out

The looks are decided: Marble table for the console, Bandung for Rabakz, Green baize for Peek Poker, Order chit for Tabao Rush, and the matte daylight kitchen seen from 60°. This board fixes where things sit on the TV. Every frame is 1920 × 1080 and live, so open it on the TV and stand back.

Reply with One letter for each numbered choice, for example “1A 2A 3A 4A 5A 6 yes 7 A+C”. Screens with a single design only need a yes, or what to change. No layout costs frame rate: these are a handful of fixed panels drawn over the game.

Proportions on this board: the options marked “golden ratio” split the screen 1.618 : 1 (join panel to seats, featured game to the others, card grid to side panel, picture to question). All spacing and type sizes come from 5 · 8 · 13 · 21 · 34 · 55 · 89 · 144 px, corners are 13 px, and the screen keeps a 55 px safe margin for TV overscan. Nothing scrolls on any screen.
