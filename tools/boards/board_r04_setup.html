Choosing categories: three layouts

The leader sets questions, categories, difficulty and teams on their phone, and the TV mirrors the choices. The hard part is categories: there are 16, and six are Singapore ones with long names. Each option is drawn below at real size on the two phone sizes we test (667 × 375 and 844 × 390), using the console's own colours, type and spacing.

Every option is plain page elements: no images, nothing that costs frame rate. Each adds roughly 1–3 KB to the phone page, well inside its 300 KB budget (PERF-020).

B · Two groups: “Around the world” and “Singapore”Recommended

FitsNo scrolling at either size, measured: at most 11 buttons per group, 3 across at 667 and 4 across at 844, every button 55 px or taller with 21 px text. Done sits in the heading row; choices apply as they are tapped.
NamesInside the Singapore group the word “Singapore” is dropped: “Food”, “Life”, “Places”.
RZ-010 “any set”Met. The group button ticks or clears all six at once; each can still be picked alone.
