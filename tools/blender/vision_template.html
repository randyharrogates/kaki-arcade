What you will have when v1 is done

One TV, up to eight phones, three games. Every screen below is drawn at its true size: TV screens are 1920 × 1080 and phone screens are 844 × 390, scaled to fit this page. Open it on the TV to judge from the sofa.

What is real here Rendered — the Tabao Rush kitchens, cooks and dishes are real Blender renders from our script pipeline, first pass. Mocked — menus, HUD and phone screens are built in the browser in a provisional kopitiam look. Still yours to choose — colours, type, layouts, controller styling and motion, on boards 2 to 5. This page is the aim, not the decision.
One evening

How a night goes

OpenSomeone opens the site on the TV. A room and a QR code appear.
ScanEveryone scans. No app, no account. Their phone becomes a controller.
PickThe leader picks a game with their phone. Teams if the game has them.
PlayThe TV shows the game. Phones show only your controls and your secrets.
AgainResults, then back to the picker with the same players.

Game 2 · Peek Poker

Memory poker

Sixteen cards are shown, then flipped. Everyone secretly picks two on their phone to go with the three shared cards. Best five-card hand wins the round; the fastest of the best gets the bigger bonus.
